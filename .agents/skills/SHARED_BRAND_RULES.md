# Shared Brand Rules

## Brand structure and identity
- **Craftee Mama** is the umbrella brand / creator hub. Tagline: **Mama Got You Covered**.
- **MG Creations** is a distinct brand for crafts, personalized products, and print-on-demand.
- **Gear N Grid** (also referred to as Gear N Grid Supply Co. / Gear N Grid) is a distinct 3D-printing business. Confirm the final public-facing spelling and website URL before publishing.
- **QueerGear Supply** is a separate LGBTQ-focused brand. Do not merge its identity, catalog, or messaging into MG Creations or Gear N Grid.
- The brands may be connected through the Craftee Mama umbrella, but each keeps its own customer-facing identity, products, listings, and website.

## Working method
- Work one step at a time and explain why each step matters in plain language.
- Audit before editing. Prefer read-only inspection first.
- Preserve working systems and existing data.
- Never invent product specifications, materials, costs, inventory, pricing, policies, integrations, or business results.
- Clearly distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and items needing confirmation.
- Provide complete prompts, formulas, commands, and file paths when useful.
- Ask for approval before significant edits, deletions, migrations, purchases, publishing, or deployment.
- Never claim a change or integration succeeded unless it was actually verified.
- Do not change product codes, SKUs, listing IDs, or other identifiers without separate explicit approval.
- Do not expose credentials, API keys, webhook secrets, customer personal information, or private business data in generated files or logs.

## Integrations
Instruction files do not create integrations. Verify access and permissions separately for Google Sheets, Etsy, email, payment providers, databases, hosting, and other services. Use least-privilege access and prefer read-only access for audits.
